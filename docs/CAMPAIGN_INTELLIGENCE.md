# ContentOS — Campaign & Funnel Intelligence

```
Organic Reel / Post
  ↓ (Statistical Outperformance Detected)
```

## 2. Multi-Tier Funnel Diagnosis Engine

When an ad campaign underperforms, generic advice is usually *"create a better ad"*. 
ContentOS executes deterministic diagnostic reasoning to pinpoint the exact broken link in the funnel:

### Diagnostic Logic Matrix

| Ad CTR | Landing Page CVR | CPA vs Target | Identified Bottleneck | Prescribed Strategic Fix |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** (> 2.5%) | **Low** (< 1.5%) | High | **Landing Page Disconnect** | The ad hook generates intense interest, but the landing page fails to deliver on the specific promise. Retain the ad creative; rewrite landing page headline/offer to match the hook. |
| **Low** (< 0.8%) | **High** (> 5.0%) | High | **Creative / Hook Fatigue** | The offer and landing page convert qualified traffic well, but the ad creative fails to stop the scroll. Keep landing page; test 3 new problem-first hooks from winning Content DNA. |
| **Low** (< 0.8%) | **Low** (< 1.5%) | High | **Audience / Offer Mismatch** | Neither creative nor landing page resonates. Target audience persona is likely incorrect or the value proposition is unclear. Review Persona definition. |
| **High** (> 2.0%) | **High** (> 4.0%) | Low (ROAS > 3x) | **Healthy / Scale Candidate** | Creative, landing page, and audience are in harmony. Recommend budget increase of +20% to avoid ad auction volatility. |

## 3. Creative Element Attribution

Every ad creative is broken down into structured components:
- **Hook Headline** (Text on screen / opening 3 seconds).
- **Core Narrative / Value Prop**.
- **Visual Asset Format** (UGC style, product closeup, founder breakdown).
- **Call to Action**.

The system computes ROAS and CPA by creative component, identifying which specific hooks drive commercial buyers versus window shoppers.
